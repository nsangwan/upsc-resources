## 🏛 Exam Architecture & Overview

The UPSC Civil Services Examination is conducted in three stages:

1. **Preliminary Examination (Objective)**:
   - **Paper I**: General Studies (100 questions, 200 marks, cut-off based)
   - **Paper II**: Civil Services Aptitude Test / CSAT (80 questions, 200 marks, qualifying @ 33%)
2. **Mains Examination (Written Descriptive)**:
   - **Paper A & B**: Compulsory Indian Language & English (Qualifying @ 25%)
   - **Paper I**: Essay (250 marks)
   - **Paper II to V**: General Studies I, II, III, IV (250 marks each)
   - **Paper VI & VII**: Optional Subject Paper I & II (250 marks each)
3. **Personality Test (Interview)**: 275 marks.

## 📝 General Studies Mains Core Notes (GS I–IV)

### GS Paper I
* **Art & Culture**: Classical dance forms, Indian architecture, temple styles, literature, and UNESCO heritage sites.
* **Modern Indian History & Freedom Struggle**: 1757 to 1947 chronology, British economic policies, socio-religious reform movements, tribal and peasant uprisings.
* **Post-Independence Consolidation**: Integration of princely states, linguistic reorganization, tribal policies, and non-alignment.
* **World History**: Industrial Revolution, World Wars, Decolonization, and Cold War ideologies.
* **Indian Society**: Salient features, diversity, women's organizations, urbanization, communalism, regionalism, and secularism.
* **Physical & Human Geography**: Geomorphology, climatology, oceanography, resource distributions, and urbanization patterns.
  * Access comprehensive notes: [Geography Complete Notes for UPSC](https://upscclub.com/product/geography-complete-notes-for-upsc/)

### GS Paper II
* **Indian Constitution**: Historical underpinnings, evolution, basic structure doctrine, key amendments.
* **Functions & Responsibilities of the Union and the States**: Federal structure, devolution of powers and finances, inter-state mechanisms.
* **Separation of Powers & Dispute Redressal**: Executive, Legislature, Judiciary, Judicial Review, and PIL.
* **Governance & Public Policy**: Welfare schemes for vulnerable sections, mechanisms for poverty and hunger eradication, Citizen's Charters, RTI.
* **International Relations**: India's neighborhood policies, bilateral groupings, international agencies (UN, WTO, IMF, Quad, BRICS).

### GS Paper III
* **Indian Economy**: Resource mobilization, inclusive growth, budgeting, agriculture supply chains, public distribution system (PDS).
* **Science & Technology**: Space technology, artificial intelligence, biotechnology, nanotechnology, and IPR.
* **Environment & Biodiversity**: Conservation initiatives, environmental impact assessment (EIA), national parks and sanctuaries, climate change conferences.
  * Access notes: [Environment & Ecology Complete Notes for UPSC](https://upscclub.com/product/environment-and-ecology-complete-notes-for-upsc/)
* **Internal Security & Disaster Management**: Border management, cyber security, money laundering, coastal security, NDMA guidelines.

### GS Paper IV
* **Ethics and Human Interface**: Essence, determinants, and consequences of ethics in human actions; dimensions of ethics; private and public relationships.
* **Attitude & Emotional Intelligence**: Moral and political attitudes, social influence, persuasion, concepts and applications in administration.
* **Aptitude & Foundational Values**: Integrity, impartiality, non-partisanship, objectivity, dedication to public service, empathy towards weaker sections.
* **Probity in Governance & Case Studies**: Work culture, quality of service delivery, utilization of public funds, ethics codes, and real-world ethical dilemma resolution.
